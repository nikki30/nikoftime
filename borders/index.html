About the map

Borders come from historical-basemaps by André Ourednik and contributors (GPL-3.0): 54 snapshots of the world from 123,000 BC to 2010. Borders before modern times are approximate, and for most of history huge areas weren't ruled by any state at all.

"What changed" is measured from the borders themselves (which shapes grew, shrank, appeared or vanished since the previous map) and explained in plain English. Sometimes a change is just the dataset renaming a state, and we say so.
