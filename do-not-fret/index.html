Welcome to Do Not Fret

A practice buddy for guitar: twenty minutes a day, a metronome, and a fretboard that shows you every note as you play it.

  1. Press “Start today's practice”. A 25-minute timer runs while you work through the routine. You can pause it.
  2. Open an exercise and press “Play along”. Each note lights up on the fretboard, on the beat. Turn your sound on.
  3. Played it clean? That means alternate picking, saying every note out loud, and no mistakes. Save the tempo and watch it climb on the Progress page.

No account needed. Everything you log stays in this browser. Export a backup from Progress any time.
